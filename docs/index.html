04 — SOS

Three seconds.
That's the whole plan.

In a bad moment nobody scrolls for a number. You hold one button, your phone buzzes through the countdown, and the people you chose are told where you are.

1
Choose your people

SOS contacts work on mutual consent — both sides accept before anyone is linked.

2
Hold for three seconds

The hold prevents pocket triggers. Haptic pulses count it down in your hand.

3
Your people know

Every accepted contact gets an emergency message and push notification. Live location is planned for a later phase.

Questions

Asked often, answered straight

Is Connex free?

Yes. Chat, alerts, the Edu library and SOS are free. Later phases like Connex Pay will charge per-transaction fees where that's normal — never for messaging or study material.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android. The app is in early access right now — email us and we'll get you onto the list for your platform.

Where does the loadshedding data come from?

Official schedule data is pulled automatically through the day and combined with reports posted by people in your area, so you see both the plan and the reality.

How does SOS actually work?

You pick trusted contacts; each one has to accept the link. When you press and hold the SOS button for three seconds, every accepted contact gets an emergency message and push notification. There's no charge and no call centre in between.

Do you sell my data?

No. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not "anonymised", not at all. The privacy policy spells out exactly what we collect and why.

When is Connex Pay coming?

Pay is Phase 2 on the roadmap, after the current modules are stable and audited. Wallets and money need to be done carefully, so we won't rush that date.
