Safety Feature

Emergency SOS.
One press away.

When you feel unsafe, press and hold the SOS button for 3 seconds. Your trusted contacts are instantly alerted with your location. No fumbling, no dialling — just hold and help is on its way.

3-second activation — Press and hold to trigger. Haptic feedback confirms the countdown.

Trusted contacts only — Mutual consent required. Both you and your contact must agree before linking.

Location sharing — Your GPS coordinates are sent automatically so help knows exactly where you are.

Security & Privacy

Your data is yours.
Full stop.

Connex is built with privacy and security at its core. We comply fully with South Africa's POPIA regulations.

Encrypted Communication

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your messages, media, and personal information are protected using industry-standard encryption protocols.

POPIA Compliant

We comply fully with the Protection of Personal Information Act. You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data at any time.

Transparent Practices

We never sell your data. Our privacy policy is written in plain language, not legal jargon. We believe you deserve to know exactly how your information is handled.
