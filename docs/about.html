About

Built here,
for the way life works here.

Connex is an African company building one app for the things a day actually demands: talking, knowing, learning and staying safe.

Why Connex exists

The day is one thing. The apps are five.

Across Africa the tools people rely on are scattered — one app for messaging, another for news, another for power schedules, another for study material. And when someone feels unsafe, there's no quick way to reach the people who matter; just three apps and a phone call that takes too long.

Connex started from a plain observation: power cuts are not an inconvenience here — they shape business, school and safety. Community warnings shouldn't drown in social feeds. Past papers shouldn't sit behind paywalls during exam season. And help should be one hold of a button away.

So we're building the whole day into one app — not a copy of something made elsewhere and localised as an afterthought, but a product whose every screen starts from how life on this continent actually runs.

Connex is infrastructure for connection: a place where communities talk, coordinate and look after each other.

Mission

Put communication, awareness, learning and safety in one app that any community can afford and anyone can use.

Vision

An Africa where every community — city block or rural town — is connected, informed in real time, and equipped to thrive. One continent, one app, no one left behind.

What we stand for

Five commitments
we build against

V·01

Safety first

The SOS button is a core feature, not an add-on. Anything that touches safety gets built carefully and tested hardest.

V·02

Education stays free

Past papers, notes and the help board never go behind a paywall. A learner's budget should never decide their marks.

V·03

Community powered

Alerts come from the people who live there. Connex amplifies neighbours, it doesn't replace them.

V·04

Privacy by design

Collect the minimum, encrypt what's collected, sell nothing. The privacy policy is written to be read.

V·05

Honest releases

Features ship when they work, and we say plainly what's live, what's early and what's still a plan.

The road

Where this is going

Phase 0 · Done

Foundations

Accounts, realtime infrastructure, push notifications and the data layer everything else stands on.

Phase 1 · Now — early access

Chat, Feed & Alerts, Edu, SOS

Messaging with voice and video calls, the community feed with six alert categories, the free learner hub, and three-second SOS — running on real phones today.

Phase 2

Connex Pay

A wallet for person-to-person transfers, bill payments and airtime — built to banking-grade standards before it opens.

Phase 3

Connex Clips

Short-form video and a creator economy that pays African creators directly.

Phase 4

Connex Logistics

Rides, food, parcels and groceries on top of the network the earlier phases build.
