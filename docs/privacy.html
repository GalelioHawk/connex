Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 March 2026

Connex ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your data when you use the Connex mobile application. It is designed to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Who We Are

Connex is a South African technology company. We operate the Connex mobile application available on Android and iOS. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@connex.co.za.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information: Your full name, phone number, and password (stored as a secure hash — we never store your plain-text password).
  • Profile information: Your profile photo and area/location settings (used to show relevant local alerts).
  • Messages: Chat messages you send and receive within the App. These are stored to deliver the messaging service.
  • User content: Community alerts, posts, and other content you create in the App.
  • SOS contacts: Phone numbers or user IDs of contacts you designate as SOS emergency contacts (only with their mutual consent).
  • Device information: Your device type, operating system, and a push notification token (FCM token) used to send you push notifications.
  • Usage data: Information about how you use the App, such as features accessed and time spent, collected to improve our service.

We do not collect financial information, precise GPS location data (Phase 1), or any biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your Connex account.
  • Deliver the messaging, alerts, SOS, and educational features of the App.
  • Send push notifications (e.g. new messages, SOS alerts, loadshedding updates).
  • Improve and develop the App based on usage patterns.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with applicable South African laws and regulations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we process your personal information on the following bases:

  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to provide the service you signed up for (e.g. messaging, alerts).
  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent (e.g. SOS contact mutual consent).
  • Legitimate interests: Where our legitimate business interests do not override your rights (e.g. improving App performance).
  • Legal obligation: Where we are required to process data to comply with South African law.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Supabase: Our database and authentication provider. Data is stored securely on Supabase's servers.
  • Firebase (Google): Used to deliver push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Upstash Redis: Used for session caching and rate limiting. Minimal data is stored here.
  • Railway: Our backend hosting provider. Your data passes through Railway's infrastructure when you use the App.
  • Other users: Your name, profile photo, and messages are visible to users you communicate with, as is the nature of a messaging app.
  • SOS contacts: Your identity and SOS alert are shared with your designated emergency contacts when you trigger an SOS.
  • Law enforcement: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages are retained to deliver the messaging service. You may delete your own messages within the App.
  • After account deletion, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a South African resident, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: Request that inaccurate or incomplete information be corrected.
  • Right to deletion: Request that your personal information be deleted, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@connex.co.za.

8. Children's Privacy

Connex is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes — never in plain text.
  • All data transmitted over TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • JWT tokens with short expiry, paired with secure refresh token rotation.
  • Rate limiting on authentication endpoints to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Row-level security (RLS) on our Supabase database.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may display links to third-party services (e.g. EskomSePush for loadshedding data). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via your registered contact details. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
