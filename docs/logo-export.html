How to save as PNG for the application

  1. Right-click on the light version logo above
  2. Select "Save image as…" — or take a screenshot of just that card
  3. On Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4 then drag to select just the white card
  4. Upload that PNG to the accelerator form

The SVG is vector — it is infinitely high resolution. If you need a larger PNG, zoom in (Cmd +) before screenshotting.
